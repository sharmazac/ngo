Before you write

Questions we get a lot

How quickly will you reply?

Two working days for anything sent through this form. If it is urgent and involves a partner community, call instead — the phone is answered by a person.

Can I volunteer on a programme overseas?

No, and this is deliberate. Programme roles are filled locally, by the partner organisation, because that is the entire point of the model.

Volunteer roles here support fundraising, events and skills-based projects such as accounting or translation.

What percentage of my gift reaches the programme?

Eighty-seven percent in 2026. Eight went to fundraising and five to running the organisation. Those numbers are audited and published in full.

Can my company or trust fund a whole programme?

Yes. Programmes run on a five-year cycle and we will show you the budget, the partner and the exit date before you commit to anything.

We will also tell you what we will not do, which is usually the more useful conversation.

Do you take press and research enquiries?

We do. Email press@openhand.example and say what you need. We will put you in touch with a partner directly rather than speaking for them.
