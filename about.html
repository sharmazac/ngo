Fifteen years of working ourselves out of a job

Openhand Foundation began in 2011 with one learning centre and a spreadsheet. The method has not changed much since: find the people already doing the work, fund their plan, and leave when they no longer need us.

Our story

It started with one after-school centre

A group of teachers and a retired accountant pooled a small fund to keep one after-school centre open. It worked — and the request that followed was not for more centres, but for help with the paperwork that unlocks government funding.

That request set the shape of the organisation. We are small on purpose: eleven staff, no field offices, and a rule that every program is run by an organisation rooted in the place it serves.

  • 126 partner organisations across 14 districts
  • Eleven staff, no field offices, no country directors
  • Nine of the eleven programs we have closed still run locally
  • Accounts audited annually and filed in public
15 Years working this way
What drives us

Mission, method and the rules we keep

Three commitments decide what we fund, how we fund it, and the date we stop. They are the same three we wrote down in 2011.

Our mission

Put long-term capital, training and administrative cover behind community organisations so they can run the programs their neighbours already asked for — in learning, livelihoods, health and water.

How we work

We never open a field office. Every program is co-designed with a local partner, holds its own budget by year three, and has a handover date agreed before the first payment is made.

What we publish

Targets go out before the year starts, results after it ends — including the programs that fell short. Accounts are audited annually and filed where anyone can read them.

The model

Five years, three phases

Every program follows the same arc, with an exit date agreed on day one.

01 Years 1–2

Fund and train

We cover full costs and run governance, bookkeeping and safeguarding training with the partner team. Nothing is asked of them financially in this phase.

02 Years 3–4

Share delivery

The partner holds the budget. We co-sign, review quarterly, and help them raise their own funding from district and institutional sources.

03 Year 5

Hand over

Assets, staff contracts and data transfer to the partner. We stay reachable, not in charge — and the file closes on the date we set in year one.
