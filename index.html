About us

We fund the plan the community already has

Most places we work do not lack ideas — they lack the capital, the training and the paperwork to act on them. We supply those three things, then hand over ownership on a fixed timeline agreed on day one.

  • Programs co-designed with a local partner organisation
  • Targets published before the year, results after it
  • Accounts audited annually and filed in public
  • Nine of eleven closed programs still running locally
48,200+ People reached since 2011
