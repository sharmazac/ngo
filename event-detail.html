Community open day

Tour the eastern learning centre, sit in on an after-school session, and meet the teaching team who run it.

Event details

Date and time
Friday 19 September, 10:00–15:00
Location
Nyaho Centre, District 2
Cost
Free — lunch provided
Places left
18 of 40

Once a term we open a learning centre to anyone who wants to see one working — supporters, prospective volunteers, journalists, and people who simply live nearby and have never been inside.

What the day looks like

The partner staff lead the whole thing. We come along to answer questions about funding and to carry chairs, but the people showing you round are the ones who run the centre every week — which is the point of the visit.

You will see a real session rather than a demonstration one. That means it may be noisy, occasionally chaotic, and it will not be tidied up for visitors.

  • 10:00

    Arrival, tea, and a short introduction from the centre manager.

  • 10:30

    Tour of the classrooms, the library corner and the new water point.

  • 12:00

    Lunch with the teaching team. This is the part most visitors say they valued.

  • 13:00

    Sit in on an after-school group — reading, maths, or the homework club.

  • 14:30

    Open questions with the manager and a trustee, including on money.

From the last open day

I had given monthly for four years before I saw a centre. I wish I had come sooner — it is a much smaller and much better thing than I had pictured.

Before you come

  • Wear something you do not mind getting dusty. The yard is not paved.
  • The ground floor is step-free; the library corner is up six steps. Tell us if that matters and we will move the group.
  • Photography is fine in the yard, not in the classrooms — the safeguarding policy applies to visitors too.
  • Lunch is vegetarian by default. Note anything else on the registration form.

Getting there

The Nyaho Centre is a twenty-minute drive from the district station, and the number 14 bus stops at the end of the road. If you are coming from Springfield we can usually arrange a lift — ask when you register.

Come along on 19 September

Free, and registration takes a minute. We will confirm by email within two working days.
